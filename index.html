Understand the process

How does LIME work? 🌼

LIME stands for Local Interpretable Model-agnostic Explanations.

① 🧍

Select one prediction

Begin with one individual prediction from a model, such as a fictional loan application.

② 🫧

Generate nearby examples

Create variations of the selected case and observe how the model's predictions change around it.

③ 💡

Build a local explanation

Approximate the model's behaviour near that case using a simpler, interpretable explanation.

🌿 Why is LIME useful?

Complex machine-learning models can be difficult to interpret. LIME helps make an individual prediction easier to investigate by explaining the model's behaviour around that specific example. It does not automatically prove that a prediction is correct or fair.
