01 · Introduction

🌱 What Is LIME?

Let's start with the idea behind this Explainable AI technique.

LIME stands for Local Interpretable Model-Agnostic Explanations. It is a technique that helps explain why a machine learning model produced a particular prediction.

🔍 Looking beyond the prediction

A machine learning model may predict an outcome without making its reasoning easy for us to understand. LIME helps investigate this problem by focusing on one specific prediction at a time.

It creates variations of the selected example, observes the model's predictions for those variations, and uses a simpler interpretable model to approximate the behaviour near the original example. This gives us a local explanation of the features that may have influenced the result.

📍

Local

Focuses on one particular prediction rather than explaining every decision made by a model.

💡

Interpretable

Uses a simpler explanation to help people understand the features involved.

🧩

Model-agnostic

Can be applied to different predictive models when their predictions can be queried.

In simple words: The model gives us a prediction. LIME helps us investigate why it may have made that particular prediction.

The scatter plot

Each dot represents a simulated example based on marks and attendance. The highlighted dot represents the selected student. Nearby examples help illustrate the local perspective.

🟢

Positive influence

A positive bar represents a feature that supports the illustrative score in this demonstration.

🟠

Negative influence

A negative bar represents a feature that lowers the illustrative score in this demonstration.

Remember: A local explanation describes behaviour near the selected example. It does not automatically explain the whole model or prove that a prediction is correct, fair or reliable.
04 · Understanding the Method

⚙️ How Does LIME Work?

LIME builds an approximate explanation around the individual prediction we want to understand.

1

Choose a prediction

Select the specific example you want to investigate.

2

Create nearby examples

Generate variations of the selected input by changing its features.

3

Observe predictions

Use the original model to obtain predictions for those nearby examples.

4

Build a local explanation

Fit a simpler interpretable model that approximates the original model near the selected example.

5

Understand the features

Inspect the features that appear to support or oppose the prediction locally.

05 · Takeaway

💚 Making AI Easier to Understand

LIME is an important Explainable AI technique because it helps us look beyond a model's output and investigate an individual prediction. By approximating model behaviour locally, it offers a more understandable view of the features involved.

The key lesson is simple: understanding a prediction can be just as important as receiving one. However, explanations are approximations and should be interpreted carefully.
